A SIMPLE BUYER'S GUIDE

Technology for work, home and learning

A few useful categories to consider. Choose tools that suit your needs, budget and access to power or internet.

Phones & accessories

Smartphones, chargers, cases and earphones for communication, mobile services and learning.

Laptops & tablets

For study, office tasks, business, programming and creating content. Compare memory, storage, battery and warranty.

Internet & networking

Routers, Wi-Fi equipment and network accessories. Check local network coverage and data costs first.

Power & solar

Power banks, surge protection and solar equipment can help keep devices available. Ask about capacity and safe installation.

Software & online safety

Use genuine software, updates, backups and strong unique passwords. Avoid unknown downloads and suspicious payment requests.

Digital skills & tools

Productivity, design, coding and AI tools can support learning and small businesses. Check requirements and terms before subscribing.
